03 / MACHINE LEARNING Published research · 2025

Breast cancer classification

I investigated four machine learning classifiers on diagnostic data and three CNN architectures on ultrasound images, comparing their classification performance.

PythonMachine learningConvolutional neural networks
Read the published paper ↗
Methods & results +

My contribution: Conceptualisation, methodology, software, visualisation and writing, as recorded in the paper.

Compared Logistic Regression, SVM, KNN and XGBoost, alongside LeNet-5, AlexNet and MobileNetV2. Evaluation used accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and confusion matrices.

LeNet-5 achieved 99.36% accuracy in the reported evaluation (155 of 156 samples correctly classified). This is a dataset result, not a measure of clinical diagnostic performance.

T. Winter et al., “Breast Cancer Classification Using Machine Learning Based Model Techniques and Convolutional Neural Networks,” Nile Journal of Engineering and Applied Sciences, vol. 3, no. 1, pp. 521–534, 2025.
